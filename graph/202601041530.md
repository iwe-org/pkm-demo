# Emergence in Systems

Complex behavior emerges from simple rules applied consistently. No individual ant knows the colony's plan. No single neuron contains a thought.

Note systems exhibit emergence: individual notes are simple, but their connections create something more than the sum of parts.

You can't design emergence, but you can create conditions for it:

- Many components (lots of notes)
- Rich connections (dense links)
- Time (let patterns develop)
