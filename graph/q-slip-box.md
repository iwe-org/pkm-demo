# 💬 The slip-box as partner

> "I only do what is easy. I only write when I immediately know how to do it. If I falter for a moment, I put the matter aside and do something else."

— [Niklas Luhmann](p-niklas-luhmann)

The slip-box removes friction. When you can't write, you read. When you can't read, you process notes. There's always something easy to do.
