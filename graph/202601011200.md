# Atomic Notes Principle

Each note should capture exactly one idea. This constraint forces clarity—if you can't express the idea in a single note, you don't understand it well enough yet.

Atomic notes are:

- Self-contained (understandable without context)
- Focused (one concept, not a collection)
- Linkable (can connect to other notes meaningfully)

The power comes from combination. Individual atoms combine into molecules of thought.
