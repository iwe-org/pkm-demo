# Sustainable Creativity

Creative output requires both generation and recovery. Pushing constantly leads to burnout. Resting completely leads to stagnation.

The sustainable path:

- Regular practice (not occasional marathons)
- Built-in rest (daily, weekly, seasonal)
- Systems that reduce decision fatigue
- Permission to produce imperfect work

Creativity is renewable if you don't exhaust the source.
