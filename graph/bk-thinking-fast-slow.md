# 📖 Thinking, Fast and Slow

Author: Daniel Kahneman

Kahneman's masterwork on the two systems of thought: System 1 (fast, intuitive, emotional) and System 2 (slow, deliberate, logical).

Key ideas:

- We overestimate our rationality
- Cognitive biases shape decisions invisibly
- Slow thinking requires effort we often avoid
