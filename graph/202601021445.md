# Learning in Public

Sharing your learning process—not just polished results—accelerates growth. You get feedback earlier. You help others on similar paths. You build accountability.

The discomfort of showing work-in-progress is the price of faster learning.

This doesn't require an audience. Publishing to a blog, sharing in a community, or even just committing to a public repository counts.
