# Daily Practice

Consistency beats intensity. Small daily actions compound; sporadic bursts don't.

The practice itself matters less than the regularity:

- Writing one note daily
- Reviewing existing notes
- Making new connections

The habit creates the conditions for insight. You can't schedule breakthrough moments, but you can show up every day so you're there when they arrive.
