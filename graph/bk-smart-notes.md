# 📖 How to Take Smart Notes

Author: [Sonke Ahrens](p-sonke-ahrens)

The definitive guide to Zettelkasten for writing and learning. Ahrens explains how Niklas Luhmann's slip-box method can transform how we read, think, and write.

Key ideas:

- Writing is not output of thinking—it IS thinking
- The slip-box is a dialogue partner
- Work with how memory actually functions
