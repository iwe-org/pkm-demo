# Structure vs Freedom

Too much structure kills creativity—every decision is predetermined. Too much freedom paralyzes—infinite options lead nowhere.

The productive zone lies between:

- Enough structure to reduce friction
- Enough freedom to allow surprise
- Rules that enable rather than constrain

Good systems provide scaffolding, not cages. They guide without dictating.
