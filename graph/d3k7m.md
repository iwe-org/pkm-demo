# Research standing desk options

Considered options:

Uplift V2 Commercial

- Price: $1,299
- Pros: Rock solid, great warranty
- Cons: Long lead time

Fully Jarvis

- Price: $849
- Pros: Good value, fast shipping
- Cons: Some wobble at max height

Custom built-in

- Price: ~$2,000 estimated
- Pros: Perfect fit, integrated storage
- Cons: Not adjustable height

Decision: Going with custom built-in. Height adjustment less important than perfect fit and aesthetics.
