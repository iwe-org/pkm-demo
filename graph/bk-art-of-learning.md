# 📖 The Art of Learning

Author: Josh Waitzkin

From chess prodigy to martial arts champion, Waitzkin shares principles of mastery that apply across disciplines.

Key ideas:

- Embrace the beginner's mind
- Learn incrementally at the edge of ability
- Make smaller circles (depth before breadth)
